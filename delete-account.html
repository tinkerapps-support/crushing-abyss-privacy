Account & Data Deletion

The Crushing Abyss

What data is stored

  • Google Play Games player profile (name, player ID) — managed entirely by Google
  • Cloud save data (game progress, upgrades, prestige level, gold, marks) — stored via Google Play Games Services

No personal data is stored on Tinker Apps servers. All game data lives exclusively within Google Play Games Services.

How to delete your data

  1. Open the Google Play Games app on your device.
  2. Tap your profile icon → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data.
  3. Find The Crushing Abyss in the list and select it, or choose to delete all Play Games data.
  4. Confirm the deletion. This immediately removes all cloud saves and Play Games data for the app.

Request deletion by email

If you are unable to use the steps above or would like to confirm deletion, you can contact us directly. Include "Data Deletion Request" and your Google Play Games display name in your message. Requests are fulfilled within 30 days.


tinkerapps.support@gmail.com
What is retained: Nothing is retained on Tinker Apps servers after deletion. Data removed through Google Play Games is deleted according to Google's retention policies. No additional copies exist outside of Google's infrastructure.
