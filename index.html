Privacy Policy

The Crushing Abyss  ·  Last updated: April 11, 2026

Overview

The Crushing Abyss is an offline mobile game developed by Tinker Apps. We have designed the app to function without collecting any personal information. All game progress is saved locally on your device.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our own servers. We do not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking services.

Google Play Games Services

The app optionally integrates with Google Play Games Services for achievement tracking. Sign-in is not required to play — if you choose not to sign in, no data leaves your device.


If you do sign in, achievement and game progress data is handled by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. Tinker Apps does not access or store this data.

Data Deletion

We do not store your data on our infrastructure, so there is nothing for us to delete. To manage or delete game data stored by Google Play Games Services, visit your Google Play Games profile or Google Account settings. For step-by-step instructions, see our Data Deletion Page.

Security

Because we do not collect or store personal data, there is no risk of your information being compromised through our systems.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at tinkerapps.support@gmail.com.
