Privacy Policy

The Crushing Abyss  ·  Last updated: April 11, 2026

Overview

The Crushing Abyss is a mobile game developed by Tinker Apps. Tinker Apps does not collect personal data directly. However, if you use Google Play Games Services, data may be collected by Google as described below.

Data We Collect

Tinker Apps does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to its own servers. We do not use advertising, analytics, or tracking services beyond functionality provided by Google Play Games Services.

Google Play Games Services

The app optionally integrates with Google Play Games Services (GPGS) for sign-in, cloud saves, and achievement tracking. If you do not sign in, no personal data is transmitted off your device.


If you do sign in, Google Play Games Services independently collects the following for app functionality purposes:


  • – User ID (Gamer ID) — a unique identifier linking your Google account to your in-game profile
  • – App activity — achievement progress and cloud save data (your synced game progress) used to power GPGS features

Google Play Games Services operates independently under Google's Privacy Policy. Tinker Apps does not control how Google collects or uses this data, and does not access, store, or share it.

Data Deletion

We do not store your data on our infrastructure, so there is nothing for us to delete. To manage or delete game data stored by Google Play Games Services, visit your Google Play Games profile or Google Account settings. For step-by-step instructions, see our Data Deletion Page.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access or delete data associated with your account. These rights can be exercised through your Google account settings, as Tinker Apps does not hold any of your personal data directly.

Security

Tinker Apps does not store personal data. Data handled by Google Play Games Services is encrypted in transit and managed under Google's security practices. While Google uses industry-standard security measures, no method of transmission is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at tinkerapps.support@gmail.com.
