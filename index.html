Privacy Policy

The Crushing Abyss  ·  Last updated: April 11, 2026

Overview

The Crushing Abyss is a mobile game developed by Tinker Apps. We do not operate any servers or collect personal data ourselves. The app does integrate with Google Play Games Services (GPGS), which handles certain data on our behalf as described below.

Data We Collect

Tinker Apps does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to its own servers. We do not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking services.

Google Play Games Services

The app optionally integrates with Google Play Games Services for sign-in, cloud saves, and achievement tracking. Sign-in is not required to play — if you choose not to sign in, no data leaves your device.


If you do sign in, Google Play Games Services collects the following on the app's behalf:


  • – User ID (Gamer ID) — a unique identifier linking your Google account to your in-game profile
  • – App activity — achievement progress and game actions used to power GPGS features

This data is collected solely for app functionality purposes — to enable sign-in, cloud saves, and achievements. It is transmitted to Google's servers, encrypted in transit, and is not shared with any third parties beyond Google acting as a service provider. Tinker Apps does not access, store, or share this data. It is handled in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

Data Deletion

We do not store your data on our infrastructure, so there is nothing for us to delete. To manage or delete game data stored by Google Play Games Services, visit your Google Play Games profile or Google Account settings. For step-by-step instructions, see our Data Deletion Page.

Security

Tinker Apps does not store personal data. All data handled by Google Play Games Services is encrypted in transit and managed under Google's security practices.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at tinkerapps.support@gmail.com.
